========================================
COURSE DESCRIPTION
========================================

This course introduces the foundational concepts and techniques of Artificial
Intelligence. Students will explore problem-solving strategies, knowledge
representation, machine learning, natural language processing, and AI ethics.
By the end of the course, students will be able to design and implement
intelligent agents capable of solving complex real-world problems.

========================================
COURSE OBJECTIVES
========================================

By the end of this course, students will be able to:
1. Understand the history and scope of Artificial Intelligence.
2. Implement search algorithms including BFS, DFS, A*, and heuristic search.
3. Design knowledge representation systems using logic and ontologies.
4. Apply machine learning algorithms to classification and regression problems.
5. Understand neural networks and deep learning basics.
6. Analyze the ethical implications of AI systems.
7. Build a functional AI agent as part of the final project.

========================================
LATE SUBMISSION POLICY
========================================

Late submissions are accepted under the following penalty schedule:

  Up to 24 hours late:    10% deduction from the assignment grade
  24 to 48 hours late:    30% deduction from the assignment grade
  More than 48 hours late: Assignment will NOT be accepted (grade of zero)

Exceptions are made only for documented medical emergencies or university-
approved absences. You must notify the instructor BEFORE the deadline if you
anticipate a late submission.

========================================
ATTENDANCE POLICY
========================================

Regular attendance is expected. Students are allowed a maximum of 3 unexcused
absences. Each additional absence beyond 3 will result in a 1% deduction from
your final grade. Students who miss more than 6 classes may be administratively
withdrawn from the course.

If you must miss a class, you are responsible for obtaining notes and
assignments from a classmate. Attendance is tracked every session.

========================================
ACADEMIC INTEGRITY
========================================

All submitted work must be your own. Collaboration on understanding concepts
is encouraged, but submitted code and written answers must be individual work
unless the assignment explicitly states otherwise. Violations of academic
integrity will result in a zero on the assignment and may be referred to the
Academic Affairs Committee for further action.

The use of AI code generation tools (ChatGPT, Copilot, etc.) must be
disclosed. Submitting AI-generated code as your own without disclosure is
considered a violation of academic integrity.

========================================
FINAL PROJECT
========================================

Students will build a multi-agent AI system as their final project.
The project is worth 10% of the final grade.

Project teams: 2–3 students
Deliverables:
  - Source code submitted via GitHub
  - Written report (5–8 pages)
  - 10-minute in-class demo presentation
  - Face-cam video explanation of the system architecture

Project topics must be approved by the instructor by Week 10.
Proposals are due at the end of Week 7.

========================================
WEEKLY SCHEDULE
========================================

Week 1:  Introduction to AI — History, Definitions, Scope
Week 2:  Intelligent Agents and Environments
Week 3:  Uninformed Search (BFS, DFS, Depth-Limited, Iterative Deepening)
Week 4:  Informed Search (Greedy Best-First, A*, Heuristics)
Week 5:  Constraint Satisfaction Problems (CSPs, Backtracking)
Week 6:  Adversarial Search (Minimax, Alpha-Beta Pruning)
Week 7:  Knowledge Representation — Propositional Logic
Week 8:  MIDTERM EXAM + First-Order Logic
Week 9:  Inference and Resolution
Week 10: Planning and Acting
Week 11: Introduction to Machine Learning (Supervised Learning)
Week 12: Decision Trees, Naive Bayes, K-Nearest Neighbors
Week 13: Neural Networks and Deep Learning Basics
Week 14: Natural Language Processing Overview
Week 15: AI Ethics, Bias, and Societal Impact
Week 16: Final Project Presentations
